Rates are the European Central Bank's daily reference fixings, published once each business day at about 16:00 Central European Time, via the free Frankfurter API. The page re-checks every 15 minutes, so it stays current with the feed — but the feed itself is daily, not tick-by-tick. Weekends and holidays show Friday's reading.

This is a rough gauge built from spot rates alone. Professional stress measures also read option-implied volatility, cross-currency basis, and funding spreads, none of which are free. Treat it as a thermometer, not a trading signal.
